{%- comment -%} _includes/event-jsonld.html — structured data for the recurring meetup. The home page emits one schema.org/Event per upcoming Tuesday; every page emits Organization. Values pull from _config.yml (site.event, site.url, site.social). jekyll-seo-tag handles the page/website JSON-LD. Dates are computed at build time, so they only move when the site deploys. 13 weeks are emitted so a quarter can pass between deploys. Tuesdays from Dec 20 through Jan 2 are skipped: those are the Christmas and New Year's weeks. UTC offsets follow daylight saving because _config.yml sets timezone. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign e = site.event -%} {%- assign logo = site.url | append: '/assets/images/bocc-logo-square.png' -%} {%- if page.layout == 'home' -%} {%- assign today_noon = site.time | date: '%Y-%m-%d 12:00:00' | date: '%s' | plus: 0 -%} {%- assign weekday = site.time | date: '%u' | plus: 0 -%} {%- assign days_to_tuesday = 9 | minus: weekday | modulo: 7 -%} {%- assign first_tuesday = days_to_tuesday | times: 86400 | plus: today_noon -%} {%- assign emitted = 0 %} {%- endif %}
