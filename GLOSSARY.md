# Buffalo Open Coffee Club

The shared language of the Buffalo Open Coffee Club (BOCC), a weekly Tuesday morning
networking event in Buffalo, NY. Volunteers, admins, developers and coding agents all use
these words, in conversation, in the admin app, in docs and in code.

## Events

**Event**:
One BOCC meeting on one date. BOCC is a single weekly series, so the series itself has no
separate name.
_Avoid_: Occurrence, event series, event day (for the meeting itself)

**Held**:
An Event that actually took place, known from at least one real Check-in or an Event
Report. A Cancelled Event, or a week with no Event, was never held.
_Avoid_: Using "held" to mean an Event's information has been recorded

**Event status**:
Where an Event stands in the admin app: **Scheduled** (planned, nothing recorded yet),
**Reported** (its Event Report is in) or **Cancelled**.
_Avoid_: Held (as a status)

**Cancelled**:
An Event that was planned but did not take place, with a reason (weather, facility, another
event in the space, or other).

**Report missing**:
A Past Event that is still Scheduled.

**Upcoming**:
An Event dated after today.

**Event day**:
Today, when an Event falls on it. On the Event day an Event is both prepared for and reported on.

**Past**:
An Event dated before today.

**Location**:
Where inside the building an Event meets: the Lobby, the Food hall, or somewhere else
described in words. When not set, it is the Lobby.
_Avoid_: Address (for the spot inside the building)

**Address**:
The street address of the building, the thing someone types into their GPS.
_Avoid_: Location (for the street address)

**Event readiness**:
Whether an Upcoming Event, or today's, is ready to be found and attended: its Listings are up,
its Location is set, and any cancellation has gone out. A custom Event Image is optional,
since the default one is used without it.
_Avoid_: Readiness checklist, readiness (alone)

## Listings and images

**Online Event Listing**:
The Event's page on an outside platform (Luma, Eventbrite, Facebook or LinkedIn) or on the
BOCC website. Each Event has up to five. Shortened to **Listing**.
_Avoid_: Event link

**Public event page**:
The Event's own page on 716coffee.club, which counts as one of its Listings.
_Avoid_: Event page (ambiguous)

**Admin event page**:
The page in the admin app where volunteers prepare and report on one Event.
_Avoid_: Event page (ambiguous)

**Event Image**:
The square image used on every Listing for that week's Event. Defaults to the BOCC logo.
_Avoid_: Cover, cover image

**16:9 Event Image**:
The 16:9 version of an Event Image, for Listings that need a wide image.
_Avoid_: Cover, 16:9 cover

**Thumbnail**:
The still image a social platform shows for a video before it plays, chosen by a photo or a
timecode in the video.
_Avoid_: Cover, video cover, cover note

## Social

**Platform**:
An outside service BOCC uses, such as Luma, Eventbrite, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, TikTok
or LinkedIn.

**Channel**:
One of BOCC's own accounts that a social media post goes to: Facebook, Instagram, Threads,
TikTok or the LinkedIn page.
_Avoid_: Platform (for BOCC's account on it)

**Social plan**:
One Event's set of social media posts, sharing a video, caption, Thumbnail and publish time. Each Event has at most one.
_Avoid_: Social post (for the set), work order, ticket
_For now_: "work order" is tolerated only for the Chrome tasks' calculated view (the Chrome
task page and its code) until GitHub issue #62 renames it. Don't use it anywhere else.

**Social media post**:
One post on one Channel, belonging to a Social plan.
_Avoid_: Channel post

**Plan status**:
Where a Social plan stands: **To do**, **Ready for Posting**, **Posted** (nothing left to do)
or **Error**.

**Post status**:
Where a social media post stands: **Pending**, **Scheduled**, **Live** or **Failed**.

**Social plan readiness**:
Whether a Social plan has everything it needs to be marked Ready for Posting, or how far
its posts have got.
_Avoid_: Readiness (alone)

## LinkedIn

"LinkedIn" alone means the social network itself, never anything BOCC made on it.

**LinkedIn page**:
The Buffalo Open Coffee Club's company page on LinkedIn.

**LinkedIn profile**:
A person's own account on LinkedIn, such as Zack's.

**LinkedIn post**:
A text or video post on LinkedIn, made by either a LinkedIn profile or the LinkedIn page.

**LinkedIn repost**:
A LinkedIn profile or the LinkedIn page sharing an existing LinkedIn post or LinkedIn event to
its own followers.
_Avoid_: Using it for the LinkedIn post that announces a new LinkedIn event

**LinkedIn event**:
An event on LinkedIn hosted by the LinkedIn page. Its URL is the Event's LinkedIn Listing.
Creating one always comes with a LinkedIn post that links to it, which makes the event visible.

**LinkedIn connection**:
A link between two people on LinkedIn. LinkedIn's own term, not an admin-app term.
_Avoid_: Using it for an Admin's LinkedIn authentication

**LinkedIn authentication**:
An Admin's permission for the admin app to act on LinkedIn on their behalf.
_Avoid_: LinkedIn connection

## Post Event

**Event Report**:
What volunteers record after an Event, including the Attendee count and notes.
_Avoid_: Report (alone)

**Attendee count**:
The number of people counted in the room during the Event. The official attendance number.
_Avoid_: Gives and Asks count, attendance (for the Check-in count)

**Check-in count**:
The number of Check-ins for an Event. Not attendance, because not everyone checks in.

**Photo from Event**:
A photo taken at an Event, cleared by the photo waiver for public use.
_Avoid_: Social photo

**Sign-in board**:
A photo of the board people sign at an Event. Never public.

## Attendance

**Attendee**:
A person who comes to BOCC, identified by their email address.

**Check-in**:
An Attendee recording their arrival at an Event. Checking in twice to the same Event counts
once.

**Debug check-in**:
A test Check-in. It is recorded but never counts toward anything.
_Avoid_: Test check-in

**Sponsor redirect**:
The countdown after a Check-in that sends the Attendee on to a sponsor's site.

**Streak**:
The number of Held Events in a row an Attendee has checked in to. A missed Held Event
breaks it; a week with no Held Event does not. A streak is **active** if the Attendee came
to the most recent Held Event, and their **personal best** is their longest streak ever.

**Celebration**:
The short message an Attendee sees right after checking in, chosen from their Streak: first
visit, top streak, first streak, tied for top, continued, or restart.

## Email list

**Email list**:
The Attendees who agreed to be emailed, BOCC's own record of who may be emailed. An Attendee
joins it by ticking the box at their first Check-in and leaves it only by an explicit opt-out.
_Avoid_: Mailing list, subscribers (for our own list)

**Date added**:
The day an Attendee joined the Email list.

**Luma subscribers**:
The people on the BOCC calendar's people list on Luma, who get Luma's emails. Kept in step
with the Email list by hand, through Luma syncs.
_Avoid_: Email list (for Luma's list)

**On Luma**:
Whether an Attendee on the Email list is among the Luma subscribers, however they got there:
added by a Luma sync or subscribed on their own.
_Avoid_: Synced, added to Luma (both suggest only we put them there)

**Luma sync**:
One Event's task of adding the Attendees who joined the Email list at that Event, and are not
yet On Luma, to the Luma subscribers. Every Event has one, even when nobody needs adding.
_Avoid_: Work order, ticket

**Luma sync status**:
Where a Luma sync stands: **To do**, **Nothing to add** (no one to add, or no Event that
week), **Done** (everyone is On Luma) or **Error**.
